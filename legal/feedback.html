🚪 Step 1 — Open the app

Pick an area to try. Each link opens in its own tab.

🔑
The first time you open a link, your browser shows a small username / password box. That is just the test door — it is not an account you need to make. Type these in exactly:

🏠 Customer I need home help Book a cleaner, aircon tech, plumber… ↗ 🧰 Provider I do the work Receive jobs, go online, track earnings ↗ 🖥️ Admin The office view Best on a laptop or desktop ↗

Customer and Provider are two different logins — use the matching link for each. You don't type any account details; the link logs you in automatically.

What should I try?

🏠 As a Customer

  • Browse the services and pick one (like cleaning).
  • Try to book it and see the price before you confirm.
  • Check that you understand what happens next.

🧰 As a Provider

  • Look at the dashboard — is it clear?
  • Try going "online" to receive jobs.
  • Open a job and check your earnings.

🖥️ As Admin

  • Look at the main dashboard numbers.
  • Find a customer, provider, or booking.
  • See how a job gets assigned and how a complaint is handled.
