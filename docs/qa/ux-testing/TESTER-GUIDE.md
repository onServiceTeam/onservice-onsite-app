# onService tester guide

Thank you for helping us test onService. This page has the same steps as the feedback page, in case you want them in writing.

## What onService is

onService is an app for booking home services in Cebu: cleaning, aircon, plumbing, electrical and more. Before real customers use it, we need people to find everything that is wrong with it.

## The most important thing

**We are testing the app, not you.** If you get stuck or confused, that is the app's fault, and it is exactly what we need to know. Nice comments do not help us fix anything. Please be honest, even harsh. Small things count too: an unclear word, a tiny button, a slow screen.

## Good to know

- **Nothing in this test is real.** No money is charged and no worker will come to your house. Missions tell you where to stop.
- It takes about 40 minutes. You can split it over several sittings.
- Answer in English, Tagalog, Bisaya or Taglish, whatever is easiest.
- Your answers go into a private form. Your name is optional.

## 1. Install the app (Android only for now)

Tap the file Ken sent you. If your phone says it is blocked, allow "Install unknown apps" for Messenger (or your Files app) and tap it again.

**Do not explore the app yet.** Your very first impression is one of the things we need.

## 2. Open the feedback page and give your first impression

On your phone, open **app.onservice.ph/feedback** in your browser. Keep it open next to the app.

1. Read the welcome screen and agree to the privacy notice.
2. Say which side you are testing: customer, provider or both.
3. **First impression:** open the app and look at its first screen for 10 seconds, without tapping anything. Then answer the questions on the page.

## 3. Sign up with your own email

1. In the app, go to the sign-in screen and tap **Create Account** at the bottom.
2. Enter your own email address.
3. A 6-digit code arrives by email. If it is not there within a minute, check Spam or Promotions, and tell Ken if it landed there.
4. Type the code, then add your name and mobile number. No code is sent to your phone.

Want to try the worker side too? Right after sign-up the app asks "How would you like to use onService?". Choose **I provide services**. You can still book as a customer. This choice only appears once, so decide before you tap. The worker sign-up asks for ID and NBI documents; Ken will tell you what to use for the test. Ken approves worker test accounts, so tell him when you reach that point. Some worker missions only work after he approves you.

If signing up itself is confusing or does not work, that counts: report it as a problem (step 5).

## 4. Do the missions, then the wrap-up

1. **Missions:** the feedback page gives you short missions, like finding a service or checking a price. Do them in the app **without asking anyone how**. Getting stuck is a useful result, not a failure. After each mission, answer a few quick questions on the page.
2. **Wrap-up:** 10 quick statements about the whole app, the three things you would change first, and what two services should cost.

Your answers are saved as you go. To continue later, open the page again on the same phone and browser.

## 5. Report problems as they happen

Whenever something is confusing, broken, slow or just looks wrong, tap **Report a problem** on the feedback page. Newer versions of the app also have **Send feedback or report a problem** under Help. Say:

- what you were trying to do;
- what happened;
- what you expected;
- which screen it was on;
- how bad it was.

Add a screenshot if you can: press **Power + Volume Down** together, then attach it to the report.

## If you get stuck

Write down what you expected to happen, in the form. Please do not ask Ken how to do it first: your confusion is the most useful thing you can tell us.

If you send feedback to Ken in chat, he will ask you to paste it into "Report a problem" so it is counted with everyone else's.

Salamat! This really helps.
