# onService PH — Tester Guide

Thank you for testing onService. This takes about 30 to 45 minutes. You do not
need to know anything technical. You are not being tested, the app is. The more
honest you are, the more you help us.

## Three rules

1. **Say what you think out loud (or write it down).** If something confuses you,
   that is the most useful thing you can tell us. "I didn't know what to tap next"
   is gold.
2. **Don't be polite.** If a screen looks ugly, slow, or confusing, say so. We
   want the problems.
3. **Write it down when it happens.** Keep the [FEEDBACK-LOG.md](FEEDBACK-LOG.md)
   open next to the app. Every time something makes you pause, smile, or frown,
   add a line. Don't trust your memory for later.

## What onService is

onService connects people who need home services (cleaning, aircon, plumbing,
electrical, and more) with vetted local providers. You book through the app, the
provider comes to your home, you pay through the app. Think of it like ordering a
ride, but for home help.

## Part 1 — Customer area

You are a person in Metro Cebu who needs help around the house. Open the customer
link.

### Mission C1 — First look (don't tap anything yet)

Before you touch anything, just look at the first screen for 10 seconds.

- What is this app? What can you do here? Say it in your own words.
- What would you tap first, and why?
- Write your first impression in the log.

### Mission C2 — Book a service

Pretend your home needs a general cleaning this weekend.

- Find cleaning and start a booking.
- Go through every step (what you need, when, where, any extras).
- Stop at the final confirm screen but you do not have to actually confirm.
- Notice: Was anything unclear? Did you ever feel lost? Did you have to guess?

### Mission C3 — The price

On the booking, look at the price.

- Was it clear what you would pay and why?
- Did you understand any extra charges or fees?
- Did the price feel fair, cheap, or too expensive? (You'll give exact numbers in
  the questionnaire.)

### Mission C4 — Manage what you already have

Go back to the home screen. There are sample bookings already there.

- Open one and try to track its status.
- Try to message the provider, reschedule, or cancel. Did you find how?
- Note anything that was hard to find.

### Mission C5 — Look around

Visit the other parts at the bottom: Bookings, Wallet, Profile.

- Does anything surprise you, confuse you, or seem missing?
- In the Wallet, would you trust this with your money? Why or why not?

### Mission C6 — Try to trip it up

- Search for something silly (a service we don't offer, random letters).
- Tap things quickly, go back and forth, rotate your phone.
- Anything break, freeze, or look wrong? Log it.

**Before moving on:** In one sentence, what is the best thing and the worst thing
about the customer app?

## Part 2 — Provider area

Now you are a provider, someone who does the work (cleaning, aircon, plumbing).
Open the provider link.

### Mission P1 — First look

- Look at the dashboard. What is this telling you? Is it the information a worker
  would want first thing?
- What stands out (your rating, jobs, earnings)? What's missing?

### Mission P2 — Go online and offline

- Find the online/offline switch and flip it.
- Was it obvious what it does? Did anything change on screen?

### Mission P3 — A job

- Open the active job shown on the dashboard, or go to the Jobs area.
- Imagine a new job request came in. Could you tell how to accept or decline it?
- Look at the job details. Is there enough information to actually do the job
  (address, what's needed, price, customer)?

### Mission P4 — Money

- Go to Earnings.
- Is it clear how much you've made and when you get paid?
- Would you trust this to pay you correctly? What would you want to see that
  isn't here?

### Mission P5 — Your profile and services

- Find where you manage the services you offer and your profile.
- Could you add or change a service? Update your details?
- Anything confusing about how you present yourself to customers?

**Before moving on:** Best thing and worst thing about the provider app, one
sentence each.

## Part 3 — Admin area (optional, more office-style)

This is the back office that the onService team uses to run the platform. It is
more detailed and built for a laptop. You don't need to understand every number.
Just react to whether it makes sense and whether you could find things.

Open the admin link.

### Mission A1 — The dashboard

- What is this screen telling whoever runs the business?
- Could you tell at a glance if the business is healthy? What would you want
  added?

### Mission A2 — Find a person

- Go to Customers, then Providers.
- Pick one and open their detail. Could you find what you needed about them?

### Mission A3 — A booking and dispatch

- Open Bookings and look at one.
- Open Dispatch. This is where jobs get assigned. Does it make sense how a job
  would be sent to a provider?

### Mission A4 — Problems and money

- Open Disputes. If a customer complained, could you tell what to do?
- Open Financials and Payouts. Is it clear what's owed and to whom?

### Mission A5 — Setup

- Open Service Areas and Pricing Rules.
- Could a non-technical manager add a new city or change a price here? Or would
  they need help?

**Before moving on:** Best thing and worst thing about the admin panel.
