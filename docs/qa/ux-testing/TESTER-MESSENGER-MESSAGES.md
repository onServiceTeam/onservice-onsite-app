# Messages to send testers on Messenger

October 12, 2026. Copy and paste. Replace anything in [square brackets] before sending.

Why these are written this way:

- **Short paragraphs.** People skim chat messages, so the detailed instructions live on the feedback page instead.
- **"We are testing the app, not you."** The research says people hold back criticism to be polite, especially to the person who made the thing. These messages give them permission to be harsh, and send their answers to a private form, not to your chat.
- **Missions instead of "test everything".** "Slowly test each area and write down everything" sounds like a big homework job, and it produces vague, tired notes. Real goals ("book an aircon cleaning for Saturday") produce specific, comparable answers.
- **No guessing.** The messages say up front that nothing is charged and nobody comes to their house, so they don't hold back out of worry.
