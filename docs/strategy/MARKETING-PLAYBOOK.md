# MARKETING PLAYBOOK — onService PH

> **STATUS (2026-06-04) — the launch market below is superseded.** The platform is multi-city and city-agnostic (cities configured in admin). Current default / first launch market is **Metro Cebu**; expansion cities (Boracay, General Santos, Davao, Metro Manila, Bacolod, and others) are turned on in admin when ready. The Boracay→Kalibo→Iloilo path below is historical and kept for the B2B tactics it describes, which still apply to any tourist market. Current source of truth: `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/strategy/CEBU-LAUNCH-PLAN.md`.

**Launch market (historical):** Boracay Island (Malay, Aklan), expanding to Kalibo (m6) and Iloilo (m12).
**Strategic frame:** B2B-first via tourism accommodation operators, then C2C via residential and HOA channels. This was settled in the April 16 strategy chat.

The key insight: Boracay isn't a typical Tier-2 PH city. It's a 10.32 sq km tourist island with seasonal demographics — ~37,000 permanent residents but 2 million annual visitors driving recurring service demand at hotels, condotels, hostels, and short-term rentals. The right marketing strategy reflects that mix.

## Phase 1 (Months 1-3): B2B sales-led, Boracay only

### Primary channel — Hotel / condotel / Airbnb operator outreach

This is the highest-ROI channel for Boracay launch and probably for the first six months.

**Targets:**
- Mid-tier hotels in Station 1 (Discovery Shores, Henann, Astoria, Boracay Regency, Crimson)
- Station 2 mid-tier (Boracay Mandarin, Boracay Tropics, Boracay Holiday Inn, Microtel)
- Station 3 budget (numerous, ~₱1,500-3,500/night)
- Condotel buildings (the dozens of mixed-ownership condo buildings — One Azalea, Boracay Skyloft, etc.)
- Airbnb superhost operators (often manage 3-15 units each — easier procurement target than 15 individual hosts)
- Hostels and pension houses

**Sales script frame:**

> "We're onService — a vetted, insured booking platform for AC cleaning, deep cleaning, plumbing, and electrical. Your housekeeping team handles daily turnover. We handle the heavier work that breaks the routine — AC quarterly cleaning, deep cleaning between long-term guests, plumbing emergencies, electrical fixes. NBI-cleared technicians, before/after photos, ₱500K guarantee per booking, transparent pricing in the app. Your team books, we dispatch, you approve completion. No more chasing personal kuya numbers."

**Outreach mechanics:**
- Walk the stations. Knock on managers' offices. 50 hotels in 4 days is realistic.
- Leave a one-page service rate card (printed, A4, no QR code clutter — the manager passes it to ownership)
- Follow up via Viber/Messenger 48h later (their operations runs on Viber, not email)
- Convert to recurring contracts: "₱X per AC unit per quarter, scheduled by us, billed monthly"

**Expected conversion:** 8-15% of contacted properties signed within first 90 days. Each property = ₱5K-50K monthly recurring depending on size. Hit 12 properties at ₱20K average = ₱240K monthly recurring within 90 days. This funds operations.

### Secondary channel — Condo association partnerships

Boracay has a growing residential condo population (Filipinos plus expat retirees). HOAs in buildings like One Azalea, Sevina Park, Boracay Newcoast, and the various Brgy Yapak/Manoc-Manoc subdivisions.

**Pitch:** "We give your residents a vetted, insured option for home services. You don't manage providers — we do. We give you a 5% kickback on every booking from your members, paid quarterly."

**Mechanics:**
- HOA boards meet monthly. Get on the agenda.
- Provide HOA admin a printable code: `OnService-OneAzalea-2026`
- Track redemption per code in admin → revenue-share calculation automatic in Phase 04 dashboard
- Provide referral kit: lobby poster (A2), elevator inserts (A4), Viber broadcast template

**Expected:** 3-5 HOA partnerships in first 90 days. Each delivers 8-30 customers in the first quarter.

### Light Facebook ads

Boracay does have FB demand but the per-booking volume is small (37K residents). The right play is:

- **Click-to-Messenger ads** to capture intent for one-off customers (deep cleaning, AC unit cleaning, plumbing emergency)
- Geographic targeting: Aklan + Caticlan + 10km radius (Caticlan is the airport gateway, demand spillover is real)
- Audience: 25-55, household income high/very high, interests in home + Filipino diaspora returnees (OFW remittance buys household services)
- Creative: short reels of before/after AC clean, condo deep clean, English copy

**Budget:** ₱30-50K/month for first 3 months. Don't scale FB beyond this in Boracay — TAM is too thin to justify.

### Local Boracay channels

- **D'Mall Boracay weekend kiosk** — D'Mall is the central commercial hub. ₱8K-15K for a weekend table. Low signup volume but high signal value (locals see the brand). Run 2x in months 1-2 for awareness.
- **Boracay Island Tourism Council (BITC)** — try to get listed in their service vendor directory. Free.
- **Tricycle / e-trike body wraps** — Boracay's main road is short, e-trikes circulate constantly. ₱400-700/month per vehicle. 5 e-trikes = ₱3.5K/month for ubiquitous brand exposure. Negotiable seasonally.
- **Cebu Pacific / PAL inflight magazine** — too expensive for launch, revisit at scale
- **Caticlan ferry terminal poster** — every visitor passes through. ₱5K/month for a 4ft × 6ft. High-funnel awareness.

### Provider recruitment (sourcing supply)

Boracay-side:
- Direct outreach to TESDA RAC NCII (aircon) graduates from Aklan and surrounding (Capiz, Antique)
- OWWA returnee programs — many returnees have OFW skills training in maintenance trades
- Facebook groups: "Boracay Service Providers," "Aklan Buy and Sell," "Kalibo Aklan Skilled Workers"
- Partnership with Aklan State University vocational annex in Banga (Aklan)
- Walk-in recruitment at Tabon (across-water mainland) and Caticlan

**Vetting process** (per April 16 chat, kept here for completeness):
1. Application via FB form or in-app
2. Phone screen (15 min) with provider ops
3. Skills test in-person (at office or rented space) for first 50 providers
4. NBI clearance verification (already in code)
5. References: 2 prior customers or 1 prior employer
6. 3-job probation period with mandatory before/after photos and customer rating ≥ 4.5
7. Tier promotion to Verified at job 5

Founding tier (first 50 providers): 10% commission for 12 months. **This is a strategic recommendation that requires runtime config update in Phase 03.** If not implemented, providers default to standard New tier (15%).

## Phase 2 (Months 4-6): C2C scale + Kalibo expansion prep

By month 4, B2B contracts should be generating ₱200-400K monthly recurring. Now you scale C2C:

### Increased FB ads (Boracay focus)

- Scale to ₱60-100K/month
- Add Click-to-Site campaigns for app downloads
- Carousel ads showing service categories
- Retargeting pixel from website to app install

### Influencer partnerships

Boracay has hundreds of small lifestyle / travel creators (1-50K followers). Most accept barter (free service in exchange for content) plus ₱2-5K cash. Target 5-10 micro-influencers in Q2.

### Kalibo prep (m5-6)

- Recruit 2 city scouts in Kalibo via OWWA / TESDA channels
- Map service-area zones (Kalibo proper, Numancia, Banga, Lezo)
- Test booking volume via Click-to-Messenger ads (should reveal latent demand without committing infrastructure)
- Identify 5-10 Kalibo HOAs for parallel B2B path

## Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Kalibo launch, Iloilo prep

Kalibo go-live process mirrors Boracay launch playbook:

- Hire/promote a city manager (₱25-35K/month base, lower than GenSan because Aklan wages are lower)
- Activate `service_areas` row for Kalibo and surrounding municipalities
- 60% B2B (hotels in Kalibo proper plus Boracay-bound transit lodging), 40% C2C
- Marketing budget: ₱150K/month for Kalibo across FB ads + tricycle wraps + radio (Bombo Radyo Kalibo)

### Iloilo prep (m8-9)

- Aklan-Iloilo ferry route enables shared back-office (provider ops, dispatch can stay in Boracay/Kalibo HQ)
- Iloilo is 468K population, larger middle class, real BPO presence — different demand profile (more office cleaning, more electrical for residential AC heavy users)
- Identify Iloilo city manager candidate
- Pre-recruit 30 providers via Iloilo State University and ICST (Iloilo) TESDA partners

---

## Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Iloilo launch

Same playbook but with FB ads as the primary channel (Iloilo middle class is more digitally engaged than Aklan):

- ₱200-300K/month FB budget across Iloilo proper + Pavia + Oton
- BPO-direct B2B (Iloilo has Convergys, Teleperformance, etc. — break-room and office cleaning contracts)
- Iloilo Business Park HOA partnerships
- Radyo Bombo Iloilo, Aksyon Radyo for awareness

## What's NOT in this playbook (and why)

- **Davao / Cebu / Manila launch** — outside the Boracay → Kalibo → Iloilo decision. Phase 3+ if it ever happens.
- **Mall kiosks at SM Iloilo / SM Cebu** — same reason, future scope.
- **TV / radio in Manila** — out of budget and out of geographic scope.
- **Influencer mega-deals (₱100K+ per post)** — not at this scale.
- **Trade shows / industry conferences** — low ROI for marketplace consumer apps; better to spend on direct sales.
- **GenSan-specific tactics** (KCC kiosk, Doña Soledad subdivision, GSNSAT TESDA, GenSan TODAs) — settled-and-rejected per April 16 strategy chat. If Boracay → Kalibo → Iloilo path proves wrong, these are warm-but-not-current backup options.

## What success looks like by month 12

- **Bookings:** 4,000-7,000 cumulative across 3 cities
- **Active providers:** 250-400 across 3 cities
- **B2B contracts:** 30-50 recurring properties (Boracay-heavy initially, Kalibo + Iloilo following)
- **Revenue:** ₱8-15M cumulative gross, ~₱2-3M net to platform
- **Geographic split:** 50% Boracay, 30% Kalibo + nearby, 20% Iloilo by month 12
- **NPS:** 60+ (industry benchmark for service marketplaces is ~45)
- **Provider tier mix:** ~150 founding/new, ~80 verified, ~50 pro/elite

If you hit even 60% of these, you've succeeded. If you hit 30%, you've validated something — keep iterating. If you hit <20%, the strategy needs serious rethinking before continuing capital deployment.
